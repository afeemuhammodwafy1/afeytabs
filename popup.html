Welcome to Afey Tabs

Save your workspace. Remember your progress.

  1. 1
    Save your workspace

    Choose Save current workspace to see your open web tabs.

  2. 2
    Choose tabs and add a note

    Select the tabs you want, give the workspace a name, and add what you were working on or what comes next.

  3. 3
    Return when you’re ready

    Use Restore to reopen saved tabs, Edit note to update your context, or Delete to remove a workspace.

  4. 4
    Keep a backup

    Use Export to download a JSON backup of your saved workspaces.

Your workspaces are stored locally in this browser. Notes and backups may contain sensitive URLs or information, so don’t save secrets.
